# MarchMadnessLeverageTool
This is my march madness leverage tool for the 2026 tournament
## Idea
Give yourself the best chance at winning march madness by gaining a statistical advantage on the field
## Key
Yahoo: Percentage picked to advance in that round on Yahoo's site
Silver: Nate Silver's Projection on how likely a team is to advance in that round
GOF: Gain on Field the difference in percentage picked vs percent to advance (statistical advantage)
EGOF: Effective Gain On Field The "If you win" effective gain on advancing this team

## Ideal Way to fill out the bracket
Find the Highest EGOF in the NCG and pick that team
Then find out Highest EGOF F4 in the opposite region 
find Highest EGOF final 4 teams
then Elite 8 and etc.
